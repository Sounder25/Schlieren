### Cancun (Target Fork)

Engine is hardwired to Cancun rules. All Cancun EIPs implemented:

- ✅ EIP-1153: Transient storage (TSTORE/TLOAD)
- ✅ EIP-4844: Blob transactions (BLOBHASH, BLOBBASEFEE)
- ✅ EIP-5656: MCOPY opcode
- ✅ EIP-6780: SELFDESTRUCT semantics change

**Expected: 100% pass rate on Cancun fixtures**

### Pre-Cancun Forks

Engine charges Cancun gas costs unconditionally, causing:
- EIP-2929 warm/cold costs on pre-Berlin forks (expected failures)
- EIP-2028 calldata pricing delta
- EIP-3860 initcode word gas on pre-Shanghai forks

**Known gaps documented in ASSESSMENT_AND_PLAN.md**

- ❌ EIP-7702: SET_CODE_TX (account abstraction)

- ❌ EIP-7825: Transaction gas limit cap
